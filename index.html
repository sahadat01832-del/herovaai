How it works

  1. Create an account and answer a few questions about your business.
  2. Connect the WhatsApp number your customers already message.
  3. Let it reply — and take over yourself whenever you want.

HerovaAi stores your business data and conversations in the operator's own database, and only sends message text to an AI provider when the assistant writes a reply. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is kept and how to delete it.
